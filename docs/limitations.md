# Limitations
1. LST measures surface temperature at one overpass time; it is not air temperature or human exposure.
2. Predictive relationships are associations, not causation.
3. Public satellite data has gaps (clouds) and coarse thermal resolution.
4. Model accuracy outside Long Beach or outside the training years is untested.
5. Existing free tools (Tree Equity Score, California Heat Assessment Tool) may already meet some users' needs; value-add is still to be validated.
