# Data sheet

## Sources and provenance

Public USGS Landsat Collection 2 Level 2 products are accessed through the
[Microsoft Planetary Computer STAC API](https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/api/stac/v1).
The pinned scenes are:

| Scene | Date |
|---|---|
| `LC08_L2SP_041036_20230712_02_T1` | 2023-07-12 |
| `LC09_L2SP_041036_20230805_02_T1` | 2023-08-05 |
| `LC08_L2SP_041036_20230829_02_T1` | 2023-08-29 |

These scenes are a small convenience sample of summer observations, not a representative summer
climatology. Dates and scene IDs are fixed in configuration, so reruns do not silently select new scenes.

Boundaries: Census Bureau [2023 California places](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2023/shp/cb_2023_06_place_500k.zip)
and [2023 California tracts](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2023/shp/cb_2023_06_tract_500k.zip),
1:500,000 cartographic boundary files. Long Beach place GEOID: `0643000`; Los Angeles County: `037`.
Tracts are intersected with the city polygon. Only polygon area is retained, including small city
fragments; no named-neighborhood correspondence is claimed. These generalized boundaries are
suitable for this display prototype, not legal boundary determinations.

USGS and Census data are public U.S. government data. No private or individual-level data is used.
NLCD, ACS, and SVI are not ingested in this MVP, and spectral indices are not substitutes for their
canopy, imperviousness, or vulnerability measures.

`data/raw/MANIFEST.json` records source URLs, scene datetimes, cached raster checksums,
boundary archive checksums, and the time the cache manifest was written. That timestamp is not
necessarily the original download time when files are reused. Unsigned STAC metadata is cached
per scene. Cropped rasters retain raw digital numbers, projection, pixel spacing, and affine transform.
Feature metadata records the manifest hash, feature-table hash, and settings; training metadata
preserves that feature provenance and the actual Python/training package versions.

## Processing

The [USGS scaling guidance](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/how-do-i-use-a-scale-factor-landsat-level-2-science-products)
specifies `ST × 0.00341802 + 149 − 273.15` for Celsius and `SR × 0.0000275 − 0.2`
for reflectance. Surface temperature is an atmospherically corrected satellite retrieval.

Used assets: `green`, `red`, `nir08`, `swir16`, `lwir11`, `qa_pixel`, and `qa_radsat`.
Exclude QA_PIXEL fill, dilated cloud, cirrus, cloud, shadow, snow, and water; require QA_RADSAT
zero. Reflectance digital numbers must be within USGS's valid range 7273–43636; thermal digital
numbers must be at least 293. Undefined spectral indices are excluded.

Compute pixel-level indices from scaled reflectance, then average indices and Celsius LST over
one common valid mask. NDVI = (NIR − red)/(NIR + red); NDBI = (SWIR1 − NIR)/(SWIR1 + NIR);
NDWI = (green − NIR)/(green + NIR). These are spectral responses, not land-cover percentages.
Raster masks use pixel centers, without fractional boundary-pixel weighting.

Keep a tract/date only with ≥30 valid 30 m grid pixels and valid fraction ≥0.5.
The denominator includes all grid pixel centers within the clipped polygon, including water.
`coverage.csv` includes rejected observations. In the verified run, 342 candidate tract/dates yield
338 accepted rows: 110 tracts on July 12 and 114 on each August date. Missing areas are not filled.

## Measurement limitations

The surface temperature product is distributed on a 30 m grid but Landsat 8/9 thermal observations
have about 100 m native resolution. Resampling creates no new independent thermal information.
Morning overpasses do not capture daily peak heat. Clouds, emissivity uncertainty, mixed boundary
pixels, shoreline effects, and selective clear-sky coverage can bias means. QA flags do not eliminate
all retrieval errors. Three dates do not establish a long-term neighborhood heat pattern.

## Included precomputed demonstration

The repository includes `data/processed/units.geojson` and `results/predictions.parquet` so the
normal Streamlit application runs without downloading imagery. Together these files are about
96 KB and contain the actual 114 clipped tract boundaries and 338 accepted tract/date rows.
They were generated by `make data features train`; `make eval` produced the accompanying
published error summaries. The source scene IDs and processing described above are unchanged.
`results/run_metadata.json` contains the original source manifest, run settings, package versions,
and spatial fold audit. Raw TIFFs, intermediate feature tables, and the trained model binary are
not included. Regenerating the workflow overwrites the included outputs at the same paths.
